# Store Operations Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

_Reference: SOP-OPS-2025. For store colleagues and duty managers._

## Opening the store
1. Duty manager disarms the alarm and records the time in the opening log.
2. Two colleagues complete the perimeter and chiller-temperature checks before
   customers are admitted. Any chiller reading **above 5°C** must be logged and
   escalated to maintenance; affected stock is quarantined.
3. Tills are floated with the standard **£150** change float and signed for.

## Till and cash handling
- Cash pickups occur when a till drawer exceeds **£200** in notes.
- Every refund above **£50** requires duty-manager authorisation via a manager
  swipe. Refunds above **£200** require a second colleague to witness.
- End-of-day reconciliation variance over **£5** must be investigated and noted
  in the cash office log before the safe is locked.

## Stock replenishment
Replenishment runs twice daily. Gap-scanning is done every morning; any product
with zero shelf stock but positive back-stock is flagged for immediate fill.
Date-checking of chilled products happens daily; items within **1 day** of their
use-by date are reduced according to the markdown matrix.

## Markdown matrix (reduced-to-clear)
- 3+ days to use-by: no reduction
- 2 days: 25% reduction
- 1 day / day of: 50% then 75% after 6pm

## Age-restricted sales (Challenge 25)
Colleagues must ask for ID from any customer who appears under 25 when selling
alcohol, tobacco, vaping products, knives, or fireworks. Acceptable ID: passport,
UK driving licence, or PASS-hologram card. Refused sales are logged in the
refusals register.

## Incident and accident reporting
All accidents, near-misses, and spillages are logged in the incident system
within the shift. A wet-floor sign must be placed immediately for any spillage.

## Closing the store
Tills are reconciled, the safe is locked with dual control, chillers re-checked,
and the alarm set. The closing manager records the time in the closing log.
