# Returns & Refunds Policy

_Applies to all in-store and online purchases. Reference: POL-RET-2025._

## Standard returns window
Most non-food products can be returned within **30 days** of purchase for a full
refund, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied
by proof of purchase (receipt, order confirmation email, or loyalty-card
transaction lookup).

## Extended windows
- **Electricals and technology**: 30 days for a refund; faulty electricals are
  covered for the manufacturer's warranty period (minimum 12 months).
- **Seasonal items** (e.g. Christmas, Halloween): may be returned up to **14 days**
  after the relevant season ends if bought within the 60 days prior.
- **Clothing (F&F range)**: 35 days with tags attached.

## Food and perishables
Food items cannot be returned for change-of-mind reasons for hygiene reasons.
If a food product is out of date, damaged, or of unsatisfactory quality, the
customer is entitled to a **full refund or replacement** with or without a
receipt, at the discretion of the duty manager.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, mobile top-ups, pierced jewellery, personalised items, and
downloadable content cannot be returned unless faulty.

## Proof of purchase
If a customer has no receipt but paid with a Clubcard-linked payment method,
the associate can look up the transaction in the POS system using the last four
digits of the card and the approximate date. A refund without any proof of
purchase may be issued as a gift card at the duty manager's discretion.

## Refund method
Refunds are issued to the **original payment method**. Cash purchases are
refunded in cash up to £30; above £30 a BACS transfer or gift card is offered.

## Online orders
Online orders can be returned in any store or via a free returns label. Refunds
for online returns are processed within **5–7 working days** of the item being
received back at the returns centre.
