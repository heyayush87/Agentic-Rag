# Supplier & Logistics Procedures

_Reference: SOP-SUP-2025. For goods-in, distribution, and supply-chain colleagues._

## Delivery windows
Ambient deliveries arrive between **05:00 and 11:00**; chilled and frozen
deliveries have dedicated slots and must be booked in through the delivery
management system at least **48 hours** in advance. Late suppliers outside their
booked window may be turned away at the goods-in manager's discretion.

## Goods-in checks
1. Verify the delivery note against the purchase order (PO) number.
2. Check pallet integrity and temperature probe readings for chilled/frozen
   (chilled must be **0–5°C**, frozen **at or below −18°C**).
3. Count cases and record any shortages or damages on the delivery note before
   signing. Discrepancies are raised as a claim against the supplier within
   **72 hours**.

## Cold chain
Any chilled or frozen product found outside its temperature band on arrival is
rejected and recorded. The cold chain must not be broken for more than 20
minutes during transfer to the correct storage area.

## Supplier onboarding
New suppliers complete a compliance pack covering food-safety accreditation,
ethical-trading audit, and product specifications before a first PO is raised.
A supplier is not "live" in the ordering system until the compliance pack is
approved by the technical team.

## Ordering and stock cover
Automated replenishment orders are generated nightly based on sales velocity and
target days-of-cover (typically **3–7 days** for ambient, **1–2 days** for
fresh). Buyers can override forecast quantities ahead of promotions or seasonal
peaks.

## Returns to supplier (RTS)
Damaged, recalled, or over-delivered stock is returned to the supplier under an
RTS note referencing the original PO. Recalled products are quarantined
immediately and must never be returned to the sales floor.

## Waste and donation
Edible surplus is routed to the community donation partner before disposal.
Non-edible and unsafe waste follows the standard segregated-waste procedure.
