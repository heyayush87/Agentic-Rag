# Loyalty (Clubcard) Programme

_Reference: POL-LOY-2025._

## Earning points
Members earn **1 point per £1 spent** in store and online on qualifying
purchases. Fuel earns **1 point per £2 spent**. Points are calculated on the
amount paid after any discounts.

## Points to vouchers
Every **150 points** convert to a **£1.50 voucher**. Vouchers are issued
quarterly (February, May, August, November) and are valid for **2 years**.

## Clubcard Prices
Members receive exclusive lower prices ("Clubcard Prices") on thousands of
products. The discount applies automatically at checkout when the physical card,
app, or a linked payment method is scanned.

## Reward Partners
Vouchers can be exchanged with Reward Partners at up to **2x face value**.
Partner exchanges are final and cannot be reversed once codes are issued.

## Lost or stolen cards
A lost card can be replaced in the app instantly or in store within 24 hours.
Accumulated points transfer automatically to the replacement card. If fraud is
suspected, the account is frozen pending review by the loyalty support team.

## Missing points
If points are missing from a transaction, the member can submit the receipt
within **90 days**. Points are credited within 5 working days after verification.

## Account closure
On account closure, any points below the 150-point conversion threshold are
forfeited. Outstanding valid vouchers remain usable until their expiry date.
